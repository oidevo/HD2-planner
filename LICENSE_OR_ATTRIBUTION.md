## Helldivers Wiki-derived catalog facts

The catalog snapshot contains selected structured facts derived from **The Helldivers Wiki** at <https://helldivers.wiki.gg/>. The site states that page content is available under the **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International** license unless otherwise noted: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/>.

Each imported catalog record includes a source page, direct URL, revision ID when available, retrieval timestamp, and importer version. The catalog avoids copying descriptive prose and media. Manual changes and planner tags are identified by separate provenance/origin. ## Changes

Structured wiki values are normalized into stable IDs and separated into facts, planner tags, and provenance. Some records may receive documented manual overrides. See `catalog/catalog_manifest.json` and `importer/overrides/`.
